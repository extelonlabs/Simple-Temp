# Setup guide

1. [Download SimpleTemp.exe](https://github.com/extelonlabs/Simple-Temp/releases/latest/download/SimpleTemp.exe).
2. Double-click it, and click **OK**.
3. Click **Yes** on the Windows prompt.

That's it. Simple Temp opens, and you'll find it on your desktop and in the Start menu.

**If Windows warns you:** the app isn't code-signed, so you may see a warning. In your browser, pick **Keep**. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**To update:** download the new `SimpleTemp.exe` and double-click it.

**To uninstall:** go to **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**, find **Simple Temp**, and pick **Uninstall**.

## Problems

**CPU temp shows `—`.** It needs the free PawnIO driver. Restart your PC. If it still shows `—`, [install PawnIO](https://pawnio.eu/), then restart again.

**I can't find the tray icon.** Windows often hides new icons under the **^** arrow by the clock. Drag it out to keep it visible.

**Something else.** Please [open an issue](https://github.com/extelonlabs/Simple-Temp/issues).
